✨Vietnam Journey – Ms. Nay Thu Hein & Friends
Join Ms. Nay Thu Hein and his Friends on a journey to explore Da Nang and Hoi An.
Da Nang is one of the most developed cities in Vietnam, known for its stunning natural scenery such as long stretches of white sandy beaches, the majestic Marble Mountains, and the unmissable check in spot - Ba Na Hills.
Hoi An is one of the most well-preserved ancient towns, recognized as a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site in 1999. The town is filled with rows of old tiled-roof houses and charming ancient architecture. Visitors can enjoy unique services such as super-fast tailoring, renowned for quick delivery and high-quality craftsmanship.
Mr. Nay Thu Hein and his family spent 5 days and 4 nights experiencing Da Nang and the ancient town of Hoi An. This journey brought unforgettable memories and experiences about the land and people of Vietnam.
