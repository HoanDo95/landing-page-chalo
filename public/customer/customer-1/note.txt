💕Journey Through Vietnam with Ms. Myat and Family
Vietnam is a perfect destination for large groups such as families. In particular, destinations like Da Nang and Hoi An are ideal for big groups as they offer plenty of places to have fun together.
With Ms. Myat and her family, there were countless exciting experiences in Da Nang, from exploring the Marble Mountains to enjoying fun games and attractions at Ba Na Hills. In Hoi An, everyone was thrilled to try a basket boat ride in the Coconut Forest. Hoi An’s cuisine also won over Ms. Myat and her family with its unique and flavorful local dishes that capture the essence of the ancient town.
